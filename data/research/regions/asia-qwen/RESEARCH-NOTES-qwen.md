# Research Notes for Human History Map: Asia Coverage

## Overview
This dataset adds 47 major Asian polities to the globe, covering Chinese dynasties, Korean kingdoms, Japanese periods, Tibetan and steppe empires, South Asian empires, Southeast Asian kingdoms, Central Asian states, and Iranian empires. 

## Principles Applied
- **Civilization First:** Cards focus on inventions, arts, philosophy, daily life, and trade. War is mentioned only in the context of a polity's fall.
- **No Em or En Dashes:** All text uses plain hyphens, commas, colons, or full stops per house style.
- **Date Ranges:** Formatted as "X to Y" in text. JSON uses integers (negative for BCE, no year 0).
- **Naming:** Standard English names used as primary IDs, with native scripts and aliases included for searchability.

## Border Precision
All ancient borders in `data/borders/asia-ancient.geojson` are marked `precision: 1` (approximate). Ancient empires did not have fixed surveyed borders; their control faded gradually into frontier zones, tributary states, and nomadic ranges. The polygons provided represent the core areas of effective administrative or military control.

## Polities Added (47 Total)
**China (18):** Xia, Shang, Western Zhou, Eastern Zhou, Qin, Western Han, Xin, Eastern Han, Cao Wei, Shu Han, Eastern Wu, Jin, Liu Song, Northern Wei, Sui, Tang, Later Liang, Song.
**Korea (6):** Goguryeo, Baekje, Silla, Balhae, Goryeo, Joseon.
**Japan (4):** Yamato, Nara, Heian, Kamakura.
**Tibet & Steppe (3):** Tibetan Empire, Xiongnu, Göktürk Khaganate.
**South Asia (6):** Maurya, Gupta, Chola, Delhi Sultanate, Vijayanagara, Mughal.
**Southeast Asia (6):** Funan, Khmer, Srivijaya, Majapahit, Pagan, Ayutthaya.
**Central Asia & Iran (4):** Kushan, Parthian, Sasanian, Safavid.
