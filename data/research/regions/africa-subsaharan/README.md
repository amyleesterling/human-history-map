# West, Central and Southern Africa research

Source-checked initial cultural research, 19 September 2026. This is a first
regional batch, not a claim of comprehensive coverage or independent review.
QWEN owns Asia. No borders or generated index entries were changed.

The evidence file maps runtime JSON pointers to source sections. Dates on
museum objects describe those objects, not the invention of a technique.
Undated traditions are not assigned artificial event years. Every card is a
whole-lifetime cultural introduction until the application supports phases.

## Integration cautions

- `benin` and `benin-1878` split one historical kingdom. The new card belongs
  to the earlier ID and does not imply an ending in 1715. Do not attach it to
  modern `benin-1945`, a different territory and political identity.
- `empire-of-ghana` means the medieval Soninke state, not modern Ghana.
- `mali-empire-1650` duplicates the later lifetime of `mali-empire`. Resolve
  identity before copying cards. The inherited Mali fall incorrectly uses a
  single year for multiple successor processes; it remains unverified here.
- `kuba` starts in the snapshot inventory at 1878. Its earlier cultural
  history is documented, but changing the index cannot create earlier borders.
- `congo` is a candidate match for Kongo, not an approved identity mapping.
  Inspect its geometry and source attributes before attaching the staged card.
- Ife, Mapungubwe and Kilwa need entity registration and dated geographical
  evidence. UNESCO heritage boundaries are not kingdom borders.
- Asante and Dahomey cards have one institutional source each. Complementary
  object research and political ending checks remain queued explicitly.
- Most new cards do not add `fall`: checked cultural sources do not establish
  an exact political ending. This is an open research field, not a claim that
  the polity continues as a sovereign state.
