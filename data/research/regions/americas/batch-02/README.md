Eleven source-checked dossiers are frozen for independent review. Nine proposed cards match existing IDs; two staged cards need separate identities: Guarani Mbya and the Purepecha state. None is approved for runtime publication. This packet is a continuing increment, not completed coverage of the Americas.

The evidence file maps 74 card claims to 23 sources and records chronological phases separately. Every pointer and source key resolves. Review must also examine scope notes, repeated source use elsewhere, identity, chronology, and geometry separately.

Inherited map intervals are not verified cultural lifetimes. Cultural continuity, refugees, descendants, conquest, and later governments are distinct relationships. No successor link or new border is approved in this packet.

Huastec/Teenek requires usable institutional source access before drafting. Banco de la Republica and World Monuments Fund pages returned empty shells; ICANH's Teyuna guide returned 403; INPI's atlas returned 502. These access failures are not research completion.

Priority deepening after the missing dossiers: Purepecha political transitions in 1522 and 1530; distinct Taíno island chiefdoms; dated Mixtec local dynasties; independently reconciled Tula stratigraphy; Indigenous-authored Mapuche and Totonac histories; regional Guarani histories beyond the Mbya; other Cherokee governments and earlier periods.
