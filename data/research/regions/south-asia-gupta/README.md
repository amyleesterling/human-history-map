# Gupta More info card

This packet responds to Amy's Gupta page example at `year=547`.
It adds a source-checked draft with three approximate historical phases,
13 substantive entries, an explicit dissolution account and 11 sources.
`evidence.json` maps each paragraph to source locators.

The date header is now explicitly approximate. The summary avoids attributing
Ajanta's Vakataka patronage or a securely dated invention of zero to the Gupta
court. Unverified conqueror pills are removed. Existing border files and QWEN
batch deliveries are unchanged. The drawn area at 547 has not been historically
reverified; it must not be treated as the empire's established extent in that year.

Independent narrative, chronology and geometry review remain pending. This
packet does not certify every card as researched, and does not take over the
separate QWEN research queue. The repository-wide remaining worklist is
`data/research/missing-cards.json`, regenerated by the coverage script.
